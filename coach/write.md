# Writing

## Task

If the learner pastes a text, use that. Otherwise offer this week's task (`weeks[plan_week - 1].write` in `data.js`) or a `write` item from the backlog.

Aim for 150–250 words. The learner writes it themselves, without a translator.

## Feedback

Give it in this order:

1. **Overall, in 2 lines.** Does the text do its job (would the landlord actually fix the ceiling?), and is the tone right?
2. **Corrections.** Up to 6, most important first, as a table: their sentence → better → why. Keep each "why" to one line, in the explanation language. Skip typos unless they repeat.
3. **Natural version.** Rewrite the whole text the way a fluent C1 writer would. Keep their ideas and about the same length. Bold the parts that changed most.
4. **Steal these.** Pick 3–5 chunks from the natural version worth keeping. They go into `brain.words`.
5. **Out loud.** Ask them to read the natural version aloud once. Then they tell you the same content without looking, in 2–3 sentences. That turns the writing into speaking practice.

Don't show the rewrite before they have seen the corrections. Don't praise generically: name one specific thing that works.

Then follow `coach/brain.md`, and save the text and the natural version in today's journal entry.
